# DIRECT OR SCUT - Chunk 1: clinical polymer refinement

Open `EMJ2026.uproject` and press Play on the default `DirectOrScutBlockout` map. The map selects `ADirectOrScutGameMode`; C++ generates the hull, landmarks, camera, lights, doors and crew at play start. The edit-time map is intentionally empty. The earlier attraction simulation and its original map remain available.

Left-click any doorway to toggle it once. Every slab retracts 250uu downward into its under-deck pocket, then returns to Z=0 when closed. A fixed optical sill changes from crimson to green when open. The query-only target is 240uu across the opening, 180uu deep and 260uu tall (local component extents X=90, Y=120, Z=130). `DoorClick` traces only these targets, so isometric wall faces cannot steal a click. Architectural visibility/physical collision is independent. No movement controls or gameplay HUD are present.

The fixed orthographic camera uses pitch -45, yaw -135, width 4200uu and a 1.15 aspect ratio to keep the complete hull in frame. Camera-facing walls use 55uu cutaways; rear walls are 260uu, with beveled shoulders and structural ribs every 160uu. Scale remains 100uu = 1m, with crew feet at Z=0.

The ring has inner/outer circumradii 980/1300uu, a 320uu radial band. All six pods have 950 x 850uu clear interior extents between wall faces, with chamfered corners. Their 986 x 886uu wall centerline footprints account for the 18uu wall half-thickness. Pods meet the ring directly at one shared doorway each; there are no two-door outer tunnels. Two 280uu-wide reactor spokes point NW (135 degrees) and SE (315 degrees), each with one bulkhead at the ring end and an open core mouth. The ninth door is the exterior SCUT hatch: exactly eight interior bulkheads plus one exterior hatch.

Each station has a distinct procedural landmark: faceted wrap-around bridge console bank, twin diagnostic slabs with cyan bands, paired crusher drums, square foundry crucible with dark mouth, cargo pallet pads with crates, and a heavy crimson-framed exterior airlock hatch. Floor joints are fine graphite seams; continuous recessed cool-white strips follow corridor edges. All lights are neutral 5800K: white architectural pools plus a soft directional key/fill. Saturated room-flood point lights were removed.

Geometry uses `GeometryScriptingCore` and `UDynamicMeshComponent`, including real doorway boolean cuts. Materials are generated by a native C++ commandlet and assigned as dynamic instances. The android adds a chrome material instance in unit slot 9. No external meshes, textures, assets or git submodules were introduced. Navigation, vacuum simulation, jobs and gameplay HUD remain outside this pass.

From PowerShell in the project root, with Unreal Editor closed:

```powershell
./preflight.ps1 -SkipSmokeTest
./Scripts/verify_direct_or_scut.ps1 -Bake
./Scripts/verify_direct_or_scut.ps1
./Scripts/verify_direct_or_scut.ps1 -PIE
./Scripts/verify_direct_or_scut.ps1 -Capture
```

`-Bake` fully loads and updates only the generated material assets and creates the dedicated map only if absent. `-PIE` runs `DirectOrScut.Chunk1.PIEDoorMouseTrace` in a real PIE world: each closed doorway is projected through the actual orthographic viewport, selected via the same screen trace used by LMB, opened through real world ticks, selected again while open, and closed. This verifies the camera-to-door picking path; physical OS cursor dispatch and human interaction feel are separate observations. The standalone runtime check verifies all nine targets, retraction endpoints, four exact unit bounds and the deck-floor trace. Checks require explicit success markers, not just process exit. Invocations have a four-minute timeout.

Capture writes `Saved/DirectOrScut/Blockout.png`. Evidence logs are `Saved/Logs/PreflightBuild.log`, `DirectOrScutBake.log`, `DirectOrScutRuntime.log`, `DirectOrScutPIE.log` and `DirectOrScutCapture.log`. The earlier source/material/image checkpoint is in `Saved/ScutRefinement`.
